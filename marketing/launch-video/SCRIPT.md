## Words to avoid

They describe things Sawa Cars does not do:

- "buy in the app", "checkout", "reserve", "deposit"
- "guaranteed", "protected", "safe payment"
- "cheapest" or "best price" (we don't compare prices)
- any rating, review count or download number
- "available on Google Play" (until the listing is live)

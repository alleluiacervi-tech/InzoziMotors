# Encar Pattern Map — What Korea's #1 Does, and How Sawa Applies It

> Researched Jul 19 2026 from encar.com product pages (diagnosis intro,
> Home Service, purchase guide), Play Store listing, and industry coverage.
> Encar = 40% of all used-car registrations in South Korea.

## 1. Trust is sold in TIERS, not as a yes/no badge
Encar has three grades — 진단 (Diagnosis), 진단+ (Diagnosis+), 진단++
(Diagnosis++, labeled "recommended") — each with its own badge on listings
and its own price. Premium tier adds photo documentation + extended warranty.
**Sawa:** replace the binary "Sawa Certified" with tiers:
Inspected → Certified (score ≥ threshold) → Certified+ (top score + full
docs + 36 photos). Tier badge on every card. Premium tier = higher
certification fee (revenue lever already in the business model).

## 2. The platform underwrites the inspection with NUMBERS
"진단 결과는 엔카가 직접 책임집니다" — Encar directly takes responsibility,
with compensation within **90 days or 5,000 km** if the diagnosis was wrong.
**Sawa:** put concrete numbers on the Promise page and car detail:
"If our 150-point inspection missed it, Sawa compensates — 90 days /
5,000 km." Specific beats vague.

## 3. "Drive it for 7 days, then decide" — not "7-day returns"
Encar Home Service journey: request → review inspection report → pay +
insure → delivery → **7-day trial** → confirm or refund. Refund terms are
a transparent matrix (free before delivery; after: reconditioning fee +
per-km charge beyond 100 km; usage charges start day 4 / day 2 by price tier).
**Sawa:** same guarantee, stronger framing. Rename the promise to
"Drive it for 7 days" and show a refund-conditions table (what's free,
what's deducted) instead of a one-liner.

## 4. A Purchase Guide is a product surface
Encar ships a static guide: refund policy → title transfer (2–3 business
days) → condition-check disclosure → insurance-before-delivery → delivery
scheduling (same-day before noon, weekends OK). FAQ + timeline + checklist
patterns.
**Sawa:** add a "How Buying Works" screen with the same section order,
localized (RRA transfer timeline, insurance before handover, center visit).

## 5. Sell side runs on competition
"Sell My Car": up to **30 dealers bid for 48 hours**; the seller takes the
best price. This is Encar's growth engine.
**Sawa (middleman version):** promise "a certified offer within 48h of
inspection" now; dealer-bidding marketplace later (Phase: dealer accounts).

## 6. The home screen is personal
Saved vehicles appear on home; AI recommends from search history.
**Sawa:** add two Home rails — "Recently viewed" and "More like your
saved cars" (same make/category). Cheap with existing local data.

## 7. Navigation splits by origin/type
Top-level: Domestic | Imported | EV·Hybrid | Commercial/Rental.
**Sawa (Rwanda):** Local | Imported (RHD) | EV·Hybrid tabs — drive-side
data already exists; most Rwandan stock is Japanese imports, so this
mirrors how buyers actually think.

## 8. Multiple purchase modes, explicitly offered
Delivery / visit a center with a dedicated manager / **video consultation**.
**Sawa:** add "View it on video call" to the request flow — maps
perfectly to WhatsApp video in Rwanda.

## 9. Financing is first-class navigation
Financing (모카) sits in the main nav; listings filter by loan eligibility.
**Sawa:** "Financing available" filter chip + financing entry in the
drawer (screen already exists).

## 10. Bold numeric brand claims at first touch
The app opens on "300,000 listings — industry #1" in brand red.
**Sawa:** real counters ("240+ certified cars · 3 Kigali centers") on
the About/Promise surface and store listing — not the Welcome screen
(kept minimal by design).

## Implementation shortlist (mobile-first, mock-friendly)
1. Tiered certification badges (cards + detail + report)  — trust + revenue
2. "Drive it 7 days" reframe + refund-conditions table    — conversion
3. Home rails: Recently viewed + More-like-saved          — retention
4. "How Buying Works" guide screen                        — support deflection
5. Local/Imported/EV tabs on browse                       — navigation fit
